Four-pass listening routine

  1. Listen once without pausing. What situation can you recognise?
  2. Listen again. Write three words and any names, numbers or times you hear.
  3. Loop a short passage. Copy its rhythm aloud, then say it again without playback.
  4. Write one sentence summarising the passage. Check it with your adult partner.

Final speaking challenge

You and your partner are planning a trip. Introduce two invented characters, give a departure time, buy tickets, book a room, describe the room, and explain what food you want to cook. Each person must ask three questions. Include a negative sentence, a plural noun, an adjective with an ett noun, and a sentence starting with a time phrase.

Adult assessment checklist
  • Meaning: Can your partner understand the plan?
  • Grammar: Does the finite verb stay in the second position in statements?
  • Forms: Are noun genders, adjective endings and infinitives mostly correct?
  • Listening: Can you repeat or explain a short recorded phrase?
  • Independence: Can you repair a mistake without restarting everything?

Review any weak area, then repeat the task with different places and objects. This is practice, not a certified language-level test.
